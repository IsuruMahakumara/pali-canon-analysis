# Majjhima Nikāya: Structure Overview

> **Traditional Structure Overview:** 3 Paṇṇāsas | 152 Suttas | 15 Vaggas


| Paṇṇāsa                | Sutta Range     | Vagga Subdivisions                                                                              | Structural Theme & Focus                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Mūlapaṇṇāsa**     | MN 1 – MN 50    | 1. Mūlapariyāya Vagga 2. Sīhanāda Vagga 3. Tatiya Vagga 4. Mahāyamaka Vagga 5. Cūḷayamaka Vagga | **Core Psychology & Structural Frameworks:** Lays down fundamental definitions of perception, defilements, the mechanics of desire (Iccaˉva), and deep psychological processing.        |
| **2. Majjhimapaṇṇāsa** | MN 51 – MN 100  | 1. Gahapati Vagga 2. Bhikkhu Vagga 3. Paribbājaka Vagga 4. Rāja Vagga 5. Brāhmaṇa Vagga         | **Social Dynamics & Dialects:** Moves the Dhamma into the real world. Features the Buddha's direct interactions with householders, kings, brahmins, and alternative wandering ascetics. |
| **3. Uparipaṇṇāsa**    | MN 101 – MN 152 | 1. Devadaha Vagga 2. Anupada Vagga 3. Suññatā Vagga 4. Vibhaṅga Vagga 5. Saḷāyatana Vagga       | **Advanced Analysis & Classifications:** Highly technical, granular breakdowns of Emptiness (Suññatā), internal analysis (Vibhaṅga), and the six sense bases (Saḷāyatana).              |
